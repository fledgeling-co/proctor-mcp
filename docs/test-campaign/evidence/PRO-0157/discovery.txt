PRO-0157 — a capture store an outside probe can find
date: Tue 25 Aug 2026 19:14:28 AEST

== BEFORE ==
  crossref.py notes: ["R4: no capture directory found; capture identity unchecked"]
  over a tree holding 59 images.

R4 tries six conventional names at the repository root — evidence, docs/evidence,
shots, docs/shots, campaign, docs/campaign — and reads no project configuration.
Declaring captureRoots in campaign.json answered this for this repository's own
instruments and could not make somebody else's probe look anywhere new.

== AFTER ==
  docs/evidence -> test-campaign/evidence   (a symlink; nothing duplicated)
  crossref.py notes: []
R4 runs. The probe walks the conventional path and reaches the real store.

== AND WHAT THAT WALK FOUND ==
The conventional walk found 59 images where the four declared roots produced 50.
Nine images lived outside the declaration and four were counted twice by two
roots that nested. A declaration narrower than the store is the empty-denominator
failure at the scale of a directory, and it is invisible until somebody walks the
tree a different way — which is what an outside probe is for.

  captureRoots is now the single root that subsumes the store.
  conventional path docs/evidence: 59 image(s) · declared roots: 59 · they agree

The gate now fails when the two disagree, and when the conventional path is
absent — an outside probe reporting no capture directory is not checked rather
than clean.
