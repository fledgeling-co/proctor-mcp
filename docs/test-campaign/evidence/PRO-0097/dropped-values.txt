this check was path-limited (`rev-list --merges HEAD -- <path>`) and read only
`theirs`; it saw 21 merges and 10 findings. A merge resolved with `-s ours`
leaves the file identical to its first parent, so git's history simplification
drops it from a path-limited listing — and that merge is exactly the one that
discarded the other side's value. Unlimited and reading every parent, the same
check sees 88 merges and finds THREE MORE, from a fifth merge the first draft
could not reach: REQ-029, DEF-006 and DEF-007's evidence, set on c7fbe295 and
dropped by b49b5a1e.
