# The figure sourcing that did not close its classes

- origin: full campaign run, warrant's class rollup · 2026-08-25
- audience: Whoever decides whether a machine may close an item without a person reading it
- platforms: n/a
- proposed-by-ai: false

## What and why
Four specifications were written to bring the warrant's classes to full figure sourcing, and all four merged. The rollup still reports five classes short: capture trust at 95.7%, evidence integrity at 91.1%, operator state at 94.5%, registry drift at 97.0% and surface conformance at 84.0%. Two classes are green and both were green before that work.

So the work merged and the number it was for did not move, and nobody noticed until a rollup ran months of waves later. The blocking figures are named — cases sitting below the effect rung on specific surfaces — so the gap is legible rather than mysterious. What is missing is anything tying a specification's merge to the figure it claimed it would move.

## Acceptance sketch
- A specification that names a figure it will move records that figure's value before and after
- A merge that does not move its named figure is reported rather than closing silently
- The five short classes are each brought to their threshold or their shortfall is recorded permanently
- The blocking cases per class are listed with the class, not only in aggregate
- Two classes qualifying for promotion stay qualifying, and the ratification remains a person's act

## Assumptions made writing this
- Assuming the before-and-after belongs to the specification rather than to a separate ledger, so the claim and its measurement live together
- Assuming a class that cannot reach its threshold records the reason rather than having its threshold lowered
