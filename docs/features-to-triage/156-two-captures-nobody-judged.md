## What and why
Six of eight judgeable captures have been compared against their reference. The other two have not, and an unjudged capture is an uncompared one — it is not a pass. This is the one pass in the lineage plane that ratchets rather than blocks, which is a deliberate choice and also exactly where a number stops being looked at: a figure that cannot fail a run is a figure nobody is required to move.

The count is small, which is the argument for closing it rather than against. Two is a morning's work and it takes the plane to a place where the ratchet can be raised to its ceiling, after which any new unjudged capture is immediately visible instead of being absorbed into a fraction.

## Acceptance sketch
- Every judgeable published capture carries a verdict against its reference
- The ratchet is raised to the count that clears, so a new unjudged capture shows immediately
- A capture that cannot be judged records what prevents it rather than sitting unjudged
- The judged fraction is published beside the published-capture count on every run
- Raising the ratchet is a separate act from clearing the backlog, and both are recorded
