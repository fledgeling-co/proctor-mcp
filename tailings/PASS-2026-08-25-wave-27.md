**A100 — the SIGPIPE test does not arm the SIGPIPE fix.** The commit and
`CASE-0808` both named `hangUpOnAccept` as the discriminating case and said the
process dies there without the fix. Measured by removing
`proctorSuppressSIGPIPE` from `Sources/ProctorCore/Transport.swift` and re-running:

    swift test --filter SocketBoundaryChaosTests/hangUpOnAccept   returncode 0
    swift test --filter SocketBoundaryChaos      (whole suite)    returncode 1

EPIPE back, and SIGPIPE needs a second write after the FIN has landed — which is
why the two-write probe measured 141 and a single send does not. The defect, the
fix and the suite-level arming are unaffected; the attribution was wrong. This
is the same fault the wave described elsewhere as "an assertion nobody wrote
wearing the name of one", made by the session that wrote that sentence.
Corrected in `CASE-0808.armedBy` and `docs/test-campaign/evidence/PRO-0145/def-338-sigpipe.txt`.
