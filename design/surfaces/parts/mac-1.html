Proctor

What Proctor does

Proctor lets a model test a Mac app the way a person would check it: read what is actually on screen, operate the controls, and look at what the app drew.

It works through macOS's accessibility system rather than by faking mouse and keyboard input, so it can drive a window that is behind another one, or on another Space, without stealing your focus or interrupting what you are doing.

Two permissions, asked once
macOS gives these to Proctor itself, not to the tool driving it. That is why they survive when you upgrade or switch the model you use.
